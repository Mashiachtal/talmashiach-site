About

Tal Mashiach is a Brooklyn-based multi-instrumentalist, composer and producer, on double bass and seven-string guitar.

For more than a decade he has played with Anat Cohen’s Quartetinho and Tentet, including the GRAMMY®-nominated Triple Helix. He has toured with Mulatu Astatke and Omer Avital, and co-leads the GTO Trio. His albums Tiyul and Who’s Around? are out on Anzic Records.
